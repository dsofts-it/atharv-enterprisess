## Staggered commencement

Rule 1 provides: rules 1, 2 and 17–21 start on Gazette publication; rule 4 after one year; rules 3, 5–16, 22 and 23 after eighteen months. This is not a single immediate effective date for all obligations. Using the 14 November 2025 electronic publication date yields planning milestones of 14 November 2026 and 14 May 2027. Counsel must verify the controlling publication date, separate Act commencement notification, later corrigenda/amendments, and business-specific applicability before relying on calendar deadlines. At the stated research date, both deferred planning milestones are future dates.

## Review before commercial use

Confirm the business entity/address, grievance contact, notices and purposes; provider contracts, cross-border processing, security safeguards and breach reporting; age/capacity and authorized patient/family consent; retention by record type; deletion exceptions and rights process; clinical qualifications and scope; staffing limits, employment vs contractor classification; consumer protection, tax and invoice requirements; applicable Maharashtra healthcare requirements.

Replace the draft agreement with counsel-reviewed versions tailored to each engagement type. Any non-solicitation or conversion terms must be narrow, lawful and fairly explained. No lifetime restriction, automatic punitive fee, unlawful post-employment non-compete or automatic enforceability claim is included. Support tickets are a traceable starting point, not a complete statutory grievance procedure.
